      How to play

      Two squads, one battlefield. Every enemy you destroy scores a point for your squad. Most points when the clock runs out wins; a tie goes to sudden-death overtime, where the next kill wins.

      Controls

      Drive and turn WASD or arrow keys
      Fire Space
      Special ability E
      Pause Esc
      Phone Left thumb drags to drive the way you point. Right thumb: Fire and Ability.

      Tanks

      • Assault: all-rounder. Ability Rapid Fire shoots much faster for a few seconds.
      • Heavy: tough and slow. Ability Shield soaks up a chunk of damage.
      • Scout: fast and fragile. Ability Speed Boost.

      Battlefield

      Grey steel blocks are solid. Brick walls break after three hits. You can't be hurt for two seconds after (re)spawning, while your tank blinks.

      Pick-ups

      • S Speed · O Shield · R Rapid fire · + Repair
      • 3 Triple Shot: three shells per shot, in a fan
      • B Ricochet: shells bounce off steel walls twice. Shoot round corners!
      • ! Overcharge: shells hit 1.5× harder

      Joining late

      Open an invite link any time, even mid-match: you drop straight in on the squad with fewer players (taking a bot's place if needed).

      Settings

      Keyboard

      Driving always works with WASD and the arrow keys.

      Privacy

      TANK CLASH collects as little as possible:

      • Your callsign is shown to players in your room and kept in the server's memory while you are connected, plus a short reconnect window.
      • A random session code is kept in this browser tab so you can rejoin after a dropped connection. It never appears in links.
      • Your settings and callsign are saved in this browser. No analytics, ads, accounts or emails.
      • Rooms and scores live in server memory only and disappear when a room ends. Your IP address is used briefly for anti-spam limits and is not stored in a database.
